## B. Where the first-year money goes

First-year awards (projects starting in each firm's grant year), all 11,150 funded firms, 2016–2026: £1.99bn. Thirds use the cuts of the employee dose table (£48,477 and £99,410).

| First-year award | funded firms | share of firms | first-year money | share of money | money a year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| up to £48,477 | 3,365 | 30% | £87m | 4% | £8m |
| £48,478 to £99,410 | 3,608 | 32% | £226m | 11% | £21m |
| over £99,410 | 4,177 | 37% | £1,679m | 84% | £153m |

Money a year divides by the 11 grant years in the file (2016–2026); the last years are part-years.
Effects by the same thirds are in RESULTS-appendix (employee dose table): the top third's employee gap at t+3 is close to the gap it already had before the grant.
