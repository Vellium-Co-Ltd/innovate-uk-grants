# Innovate UK study: why funded firms survive more (EXPLORATORY)

Not in the plan (v2.2); added after all results were seen. Survival as in RESULTS-v2 (open at the end of the grant year, both sides), on the same matched sets. Intervals: 500 bootstrap draws of matched sets.

## When first projects end

Of 11,150 funded firms, the share whose first-year projects had all ended by: t+0 15%; t+1 73%; t+2 91%; t+3 97%.
